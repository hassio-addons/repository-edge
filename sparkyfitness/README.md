Self-hosted food, exercise, sleep and body measurement tracker.

## About

SparkyFitness is a self-hosted alternative to MyFitnessPal. It tracks what you
eat, the exercise you do, how you sleep, your water intake and your body
measurements, and shows you where all of that is heading.

It looks food up in Open Food Facts, USDA and other databases, can scan a
barcode, and takes in the steps, workouts and sleep your phone collects
through its mobile app. Everything it keeps stays in this app.

Home Assistant signs you in when you open it from the sidebar, and every Home
Assistant user gets a diary of their own.
